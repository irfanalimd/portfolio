B · AEROBAT

Aerobat

A flapping-wing micro aerial vehicle

Watch it fly
  • Implemented Visual-Inertial Odometry system on a flapping-wing aerial robot, providing pose estimation feedback to the PID flight controller.
  • Compared the odometry from ORB-SLAM 3 and RTAB-Map, and robustness to rapid motion characteristics of flapping-wing flight.
  • Conducted calibration of the Arducam global shutter camera and Adafruit 9-DoF IMU, while benchmarking the SLAM results with Mocap (OptiTrack Motion Capture) system using the Evo evaluation package.
